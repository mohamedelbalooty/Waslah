### Revenue Projections · Unit Economics · Path to Profitability

> **Read this with:** `Waslah_Market_Sizing.md` (SOM inputs) · SRS pricing tiers (Starter $49 / Growth $149+1% / Pro $399+0.5%)  
> **Status:** Founder-built model for seed/Series A conversations. Validate against actuals after Month 3.

## 1. Pricing Model Recap (from SRS)

| Plan | Monthly Fee | Conversation Limit | Success Fee | Target Customer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49/month | 500 conversations | None | Early adopters, low-volume testing |
| Growth | $149/month | 2,000 conversations | 1% of attributed revenue | Active merchants, $5–20K/month GMV |
| Pro | $399/month | 10,000 conversations | 0.5% of attributed revenue | High-volume merchants, >$20K/month GMV |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Chains, agencies, >10 stores |

**Currency:** Plans billed in USD; invoices in workspace currency at month-end rate (A1).  
**Trial:** 14-day free trial, no credit card required (RES-20).

## 4. Unit Economics

### 4.1 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

| Channel | Y1 CAC | Y2 CAC | Y3 CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct sales (founder-led) | $180 | $250 | $300 |
| Inside sales (hired reps) | — | $350 | $400 |
| Partner / agency | $80 | $100 | $120 |
| **Blended CAC** | **$160** | **$230** | **$240** |

*CAC includes: sales rep time/cost, demo/trial infra, onboarding cost, first-month support.*

### 4.2 Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

| Metric | Y1 Cohort | Y2 Cohort | Y3 Cohort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average MRR at acquisition | $65 | $100 | $140 |
| Expected lifetime (months) | 1/churn = 29 | 33 | 40 |
| Gross margin (see §5) | 72% | 75% | 78% |
| **LTV** | **$1,358** | **$2,475** | **$4,368** |

### 4.3 LTV:CAC Ratio

| | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| LTV | $1,358 | $2,475 | $4,368 |
| CAC | $160 | $230 | $240 |
| **LTV:CAC** | **8.5x** | **10.8x** | **18.2x** |
| CAC Payback (months) | 3.2 | 3.1 | 2.2 |

> LTV:CAC > 3x is the SaaS benchmark for a healthy business. Waslah's model is inherently high-LTV because the success fee grows with the merchant — a merchant who scales from $3K to $10K monthly GMV increases their success fee contribution without any re-selling effort.

## 8. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Y3 Merchants | Y3 Revenue | Y3 EBITDA | Break-even |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bear** (5% churn, slow partner ramp) | 950 | $1.4M | ($250K) | Month 42+ |
| **Base** (3% churn, partners from Y2) | 1,700 | $3.1M | $790K | Month 30 |
| **Bull** (2% churn, Salla partnership) | 2,800 | $5.2M | $1.9M | Month 22 |

**Key sensitivities (ranked by impact):**
1. Monthly churn (±1% = ±$600K ARR in Y3)
2. CAC payback (partner channel efficiency)
3. Success fee adoption (Growth/Pro upgrade rate)
4. LLM cost trajectory (falling rapidly — tailwind)
